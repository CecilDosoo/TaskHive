# Where to Find Variables Tab in Railway

Based on the settings options you see, here's where to find Variables:

## ✅ Variables Tab is NOT in Settings

**Variables is a separate TAB at the top**, not inside Settings!

### How to Find Variables:

1. **You're currently in Settings tab** (that's why you see Source, Networking, Build, etc.)
2. **Look at the TOP of the page** - you should see multiple tabs:
   - **Deployments** (shows your deployments)
   - **Variables** ← **CLICK THIS!**
   - **Metrics** (shows stats)
   - **Settings** (you're here now)

3. **Click the "Variables" tab** (should be next to "Deployments" and "Settings")

4. **That's where you add DATABASE_URL!**

---

## Alternative: Variables Might Be in "Deploy" Section

If you don't see a Variables tab at the top:

1. **In Settings tab**, click **"Deploy"** from the list
2. **Look for "Environment Variables" or "Variables"** section
3. **Add DATABASE_URL there**

---

## Or: Check "Config-as-code" Section

1. **In Settings tab**, click **"Config-as-code"**
2. **Look for variable configuration there**
3. **Add DATABASE_URL if you see that option**

---

## Quick Guide: Find Variables

### Step 1: Go Back to Service View

1. **If you're in Settings**, click the **back arrow** or click the service name at the top
2. **You should see the main service page** with tabs at the top

### Step 2: Look for Variables Tab

**At the TOP of the service page, you should see tabs like:**
```
[Deployments] [Variables] [Metrics] [Settings]
                ^^^^^^^^^^
                Click this one!
```

### Step 3: If Variables Tab Doesn't Exist

**Try this:**
1. **Click "Deployments" tab** first
2. **Then look for "Variables" tab** next to it
3. **Or check Settings → Deploy section**

---

## What You Should See in Variables Tab

When you find Variables tab, you should see:
- A list of environment variables (might be empty)
- A **"+ New Variable"** or **"Add Variable"** button
- Or a table with "Name" and "Value" columns
